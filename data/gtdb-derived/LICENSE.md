# GTDB-derived tables

Tables in this folder are derived from the Genome Taxonomy Database release
R11-RS232 (232.0), released April 15, 2026. Attribution: Genome Taxonomy Database,
https://gtdb.ecogenomic.org/ and https://data.gtdb.ecogenomic.org/.

The tables and their modifications are available under Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/.

Modifications: assembly identifiers are normalised, labelled assemblies are
mapped to species representatives, taxonomic groups are assigned to nested
folds, and distances between representatives are calculated. Local individual
records are in the ignored `local/` folder. Published files here are aggregate
summaries and notices. The code remains under Apache-2.0.
